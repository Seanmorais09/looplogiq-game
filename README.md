# 🎮 LoopLogiQ: Business Automation Runner (Hoop Hopper)

An interactive, dark glassmorphic 2D runner game built for **LoopLogiQ**!

Play as the Owner of LoopLogiQ! Leap through glowing **LoopLogiQ Infinity Hoops**, automate incoming business chaos, and save thousands of hours & dollars for your business!

## ✨ Features
- 🏢 **Personalized Business Input**: Players enter their business name before playing.
- 👤 **Owner Avatar Sprite**: Features the Build with Gemini avatar in a glowing purple aura.
- 🔊 **Synthesized Web Audio API FX**: Built-in jump, hoop chime, and victory audio.
- 📈 **Dynamic Business Metrics**: Tracks Hours Saved (`⏱️ Hrs`), Cost Saved (`💰 $$`), and `412% ROI`.
- 📋 **High-Converting Lead Form**: Victory modal enables players to schedule a 20-min automation audit directly on `www.looplogiq.com`.
